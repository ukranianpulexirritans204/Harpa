## 🎨 Getting Started with Your First Document

### 📝 Create a New Notebook

1. Click the **"New Document"** button in the top-left corner
2. Give your document a title
3. Choose a template (Blank, Research Paper, or Presentation)

### 📥 Import Your Content

- **From AI Tools:** Copy and paste AI-generated text directly into Harpa
- **From Files:** Use "File → Import" to bring in text files or web pages
- **From Scratch:** Simply start typing in the editor

### ➕ Add Mathematical Formulas

1. Place your cursor where you want the formula
2. Click the **"Σ"** button in the toolbar
3. Type your formula using LaTeX syntax (e.g., `x^2 + y^2 = z^2`)
4. Press **Enter** to see it rendered beautifully

### 🔗 Insert Diagrams

1. Click the **"◈"** button in the toolbar
2. Choose a diagram type (flowchart, sequence, or class diagram)
3. Type your diagram code in Mermaid format
4. Click **Preview** to see it rendered

### 📄 Export Your Work

When you're done:
1. Click **"File"** in the top menu
2. Select **"Export to PDF"** or **"Print"**
3. Choose your paper size (A4 is default)
4. Click **"Save"** or **"Print"**

## 🛠️ Common Tasks & Tips

### 💾 Auto-Save

Harpa automatically saves your work every 30 seconds. You'll see a small checkmark appear when your changes are saved.

### 🔍 Searching Your Notes

- Use **Ctrl + F** to find text within the current document
- Use the search icon in the sidebar to search across all documents

### 🎨 Customizing Your Workspace

- **Theme:** Go to "Settings → Appearance" to switch between Light and Dark modes
- **Font Size:** Use **Ctrl + Plus/Minus** to zoom in and out
- **Toolbar Layout:** Drag and drop buttons to customize your toolbar

### 📤 Sharing Your Work

Export your document as PDF and share it with colleagues, or save it as HTML to publish online.
